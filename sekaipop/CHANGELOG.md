# Changelog
The full notes for every change are in docs/CHANGELOG-DETAILED.md.

## 1.1.0 - Bar pills, new widgets and phone mirror
- Lyrics: a Whole screen look, kinetic typography over the entire desktop: every line is a poster (giant word, stack, slash, burst, rows, rainbow), every word slams in as it is sung. Click-through, under your windows; `ipc lyrics kinetic on`.
- Lyrics: two more Whole screen styles, **Wall** (the whole song on one tilted wall, the camera flies from word to word, far lines are smaller and blurrier) and **Marquee** (one big row that follows the voice and fills with colour as it is sung, between rows of faint type sliding past). Settings -> Lyrics -> Whole screen style, `ipc lyrics style poster|wall|marquee`.
- Bar pills: Screen time, Timer and Network speed, each with its own card.
- Bar: the CPU & RAM card shows CPU and GPU temperatures and disk space; hover tags name every bar button.
- New desktop widgets: Downloader (copy a link, press a key, get the file) and Water tank.
- New 1x1 widgets: a 1x1 size for the User, Weather, Calendar and Palette cards.
- Phone mirror: your Android phone, live, on a phone on the screen (scrcpy, over USB or Wi-Fi).
- Manga reader and Novel reader: two side panels in your theme (SUPER + CTRL + M / N).
- Novel reader: chapter dates from the source, "chapters from <source>", select chapters, mark all as read / unread, and Continue, like the manga reader.
- Manga and novel readers: going into Edit Desktop minimizes both panels at once.
- Novel reader: installed extensions are listed first in the Extensions tab, like the manga panel.
- Novel reader: LNReader's plugins are the sources; the Source key goes through them, and a Sources page (Extensions, Sources, Repositories) adds, updates and removes them.
- Manga reader: log in with AniList, track chapters as you read, and see your AniList lists in Library.
- Manga reader: AniList profile page, an Info panel for every title and search filters like Seanime's.
- Manga reader: WeebCentral, MangaDex and Suwayomi as sources, with Suwayomi's extensions managed inside the panel.
- Manga reader: seven reading modes, read / unread marks, and Continue opens the page you stopped on.
- Novel reader: Info panel and score / year / tracked stamps on a novel's page, like the manga panel; Library covers show the source tag and the title the way the manga Saved grid does.
- Novel reader: Discover covers show the type as LN (not LIGHT NOVEL), so the score is no longer hidden behind it; titles sit over the art like the manga grid, without the volumes and chapters line.
- Novel reader: bars and cursor hide while reading, Scroll and Paged modes, background colours, type size keys, and the next chapter is fetched ahead.
- Novel reader: AniList tracking for novels only, with the same login as the manga reader.
- Novel reader: Discover, AniList's novels with filters (genre, tags, origin, length, status, score, year) and sorts; a title opens its AniList page and finds the novel in your source.
- Manga and novel readers: full window, minimize and close keys, and a smooth change between card and window.
- Plugins: desktop widgets and bar entries that live in their own folder.
- Plugins: get them and keep them up to date from Settings.
- Themes: one theme per monitor, Matugen controls, and the Custom palette is saved per preset.
- Presets: undo, redo and undelete.
- Edit Desktop: drag a widget up off the shelf and drop it where it should go.
- Keybinds panel: change every shortcut on a keyboard slab.
- Pictures: frame the banner, the avatar and the phone wallpaper.
- Wallpapers: Wallhaven as a second source.
- Quick Panel: a wallpaper for the phone, and Peek first (the hot edge raises only the tip).
- Lockscreen editor: seven new widgets (Greeting, Sun, Timer, Notifications, Next event, To-do, Countdown); the ones about you show counts, not words, unless you turn Details on.
- Quick Panel: Wi-Fi and Bluetooth lists scan by themselves; pair, connect and forget devices.
- Screen corners: scroll in a top corner to change the volume or the brightness.
- Lockscreen: keyboard layout tag, optional lyrics and optional fingerprint unlock.
- Lockscreen editor: opens and closes like Edit Desktop (the card shrinks in from the whole screen), round card corners, Lyrics as a widget of its own, and User, Date, Weather, Battery and Now playing are one size.
- Idle & sleep: choose when the screen locks, turns off and the machine sleeps.
- App fonts: pick the font of your GTK, Qt and KDE apps.
- Apps: list the apps you open most first.
- Notifications: low-battery warnings, and a click runs the app's default action.
- Lyrics widget: sync keys are icon buttons under the song title.
- Recording falls back to VAAPI or software when NVENC doesn't work.
- Displays: switch a screen you turned off back on.
- The installer sets up scrcpy, yt-dlp, gallery-dl and Suwayomi for you.
- Updates restart the shell, and Update now works with a symlinked folder.
- Bug fixes.

## 1.0.0 - First release
- Themes: new Monochrome theme and a Custom color theme (make your own palette).
- Windows cursor porter: import .cur / .ani packs (animated too); they show up in Settings > Icons & cursor.
- Desktop widgets: Lyrics, Sticky Notes, Custom Image and Color Palette.
- Capsule bar and dock app style.
- Dock tools.
- Handheld EQ.
- The shell tells you when there is an update.
- Wallpaper folder picker.
- The power button menu now sits at the side of the phone.
- Configurable monitor displays via nwg-displays.
- On-screen keyboard.
- UI consistency adjustments.
- Bug fixes.

- Screen corners: **scroll the wheel in a top corner to change the volume or the brightness** (issue #141, the way end-4's dots do it). Push the pointer into the top-left or top-right corner of a screen and scroll (a mouse wheel, or two fingers on a touchpad): up is more, down is less, 5 % a step, with the on-screen display showing the change. The left corner is the volume and the right one the brightness; Settings > General > Screen corners switches it on and can swap them (`ipc call options set corners.scroll true`, `corners.swap`). Off by default: each corner is a strip 140 px wide and 4 px deep along the top edge of the screen, which takes only the wheel (no clicks, no hover) but does cover the top pixel rows next to the corners, where a maximized window's close button sits. A bar on the top, left or right edge gives up that spot of its own input so the wheel reaches the corner. Nothing in Game Mode. (`services/CornerScrollService.qml`, `modules/sekai/screenCorners/CornerScroll.qml`)
